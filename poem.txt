Twinkle

Space is a vast place,
mostly empty space.
Yet at some point
Here we are at a flashpoint.

I look out into the distance.
The flash white of resistance
stares back at me with a flash.
And places me into unexistence.

Such is fate
As humans escalate,
in an eternal
state of struggle.

----------
Free form is still one hell of a style.


----------
The distant Mars orbiter,
MAVEN, has gone silent, lost from sight.
The Deep Space Network sends its call,
Searching through the dark of night.

A twist of spin, a fractured path,
Where is the signal, weak and small?
The engineers persist and watch,
To bring the voice of Mars back to us all.
